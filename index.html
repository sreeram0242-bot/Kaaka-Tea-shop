Our Heritage

The KAAKA Story

Founded with a commitment to authenticity and innovation, KAAKA TEA SHOP brings together traditional clay kulhad tea brewing and modern artisanal tea mixology.

We ethically source our single-estate leaves directly from high-altitude tea gardens in Assam, Fujian, and Kerala, crushing whole spices in-house every morning to deliver unmatched flavor and aroma.

Whole Leaf Quality

No artificial dust or additives. Only hand-picked whole tea leaves.

Hand-Ground Spices

Cardamom, ginger, and saffron crushed daily in mortar & pestle.

Eco-Friendly Kulhads

Served in handcrafted biodegradable clay pots for authentic earthy aroma.

Community Lounge

A welcoming cozy ambient space to relax, work, and enjoy tea time.
